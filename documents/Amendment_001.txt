AMENDMENT TO MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

Amendment Number: AMD-2024-001
Original Agreement: MSA-2024-0042
Effective Date: July 1, 2024

Between: Acme Consulting LLC ("Vendor") and TechCorp Industries Inc. ("Client")

WHEREAS, the parties entered into the Master Service Agreement MSA-2024-0042
dated January 15, 2024; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to amend certain terms of the Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 4.2 (QUARTERLY FEE CAP)
   Section 4.2 of the Agreement is hereby amended to read:
   "Total fees shall not exceed $55,000 per quarter."
   
   This replaces the original cap of $45,000 per quarter.

2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 2 (TERM)
   The initial term is extended by six (6) additional months, through
   July 14, 2025.

3. NO OTHER CHANGES
   All other terms and conditions of the original Agreement shall remain
   in full force and effect.

4. ENTIRE AMENDMENT
   This Amendment, together with the original Agreement, constitutes the
   entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Amendment as of the
Effective Date set forth above.
