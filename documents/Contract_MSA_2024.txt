MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

Between: Acme Consulting LLC ("Vendor") and TechCorp Industries Inc. ("Client")
Effective Date: January 15, 2024
Agreement ID: MSA-2024-0042

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Vendor shall provide strategic consulting services including but not limited to:
technology assessment, process optimization, and digital transformation advisory.

SECTION 2: TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of
twelve (12) months, automatically renewing for successive one-year terms unless
terminated by either party with sixty (60) days written notice.

SECTION 3: PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 All invoices shall be payable within Net 30 days of receipt.
3.2 Late payments shall incur interest at 1.5% per month.
3.3 The Client shall pay all undisputed invoices in full.

SECTION 4: FEES AND CAPS
4.1 The Vendor's standard hourly rate is $100 per hour.
4.2 Total fees shall not exceed $45,000 per quarter (the "Quarterly Fee Cap").
4.3 Any work exceeding the Quarterly Fee Cap requires prior written approval.

SECTION 5: TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with sixty (60) days written notice.
5.2 Upon termination, the Client shall pay for all services rendered through the
    effective date of termination.
5.3 Sections 6, 7, and 8 shall survive termination.

SECTION 6: CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information
disclosed during the course of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

SECTION 7: LIABILITY
7.1 Liability is capped at two times (2x) the Quarterly Fee Cap ($90,000).
7.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

SECTION 8: GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

SECTION 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration under the rules of the
American Arbitration Association in Wilmington, Delaware.
